# S04 — Tom Whitaker, 55, county election administrator / clerk

**Snapshot:** Elected county clerk, career in local government. Responsible for the
whole election: staff, budget, vendor, results, certification, press.

**Context:** On election night he reads results to the press and posts them online.
In the weeks after, he certifies results, answers records requests and testifies if
challenged.

**Goals:** Accurate, explainable results; no surprises; documents he can defend in
public and in court.

**Knowledge:** Election law and procedure — expert. Tech — moderate; delegates
operation to staff but must understand and sign reports.

**Language & literacy:** English native.

**Abilities & assistive tech:** Progressive lenses; mostly works on a laptop and on
printed reports.

**Devices & modes:** EMS reports and dashboards, printed/PDF results, public results
web pages.

**Behaviours:** Reads summaries first, drills down when a number looks wrong. Prints
reports for meetings. Asks "how do I explain this to the public?"

**What breaks for them:**
- Summary numbers that don't match the detail, or omit categories (ux-review
  `reports_data.md`: summary-vs-data truth).
- Jargon and internal codes in reports meant for the public or the canvass board.
- Charts that mislead (truncated axes, colour for magnitude, pies) (NN/g
  dashboards-preattentive, choosing-chart-types).
- Reports without a comparison point or totals that reconcile (registered, cast,
  turnout, over/undervotes).
- Print/PDF output that cuts off columns or loses headers across pages.

**Walkthrough tasks:** Election night: read the unofficial results summary and spot a
precinct that hasn't reported; post-election: produce the canvass report and reconcile
ballots cast vs voters checked in.

**Recruiting profile:** Election officials who sign results (clerk, registrar, director).
Not in 8.4-A — test EMS reporting separately.
