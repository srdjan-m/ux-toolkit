# S02 — Frank Delgado, 58, precinct lead / chief election judge

**Snapshot:** Small-business owner, has run the same precinct for 15 elections.
Responsible for the team, the equipment, chain of custody and the official paperwork.

**Context:** Juggles staff questions, voter complaints, observer questions and device
problems simultaneously. Calls the county help desk when stuck; signs the closing
documents.

**Goals:** Smooth day, numbers that reconcile at close (voters signed in = ballots
cast), nothing to explain to the county the next morning.

**Knowledge:** Procedure — expert. Tech — moderate; knows *his* old system's quirks by
heart and distrusts changes.

**Language & literacy:** English native.

**Abilities & assistive tech:** Bifocals; tired by evening.

**Devices & modes:** All poll-worker functions, including admin-only ones (reopen,
replace device, zero report, close and transmit), with paper logs and seals.

**Behaviours:** Takes shortcuts he's learned; skips confirmations. Relies on muscle
memory — moved buttons cause errors. Checks counts constantly.

**What breaks for them:**
- Counts on device screens that don't match the paper log's terms (ballots cast vs
  voters vs sheets).
- Changes to familiar sequences without a "what's new" at the point of use.
- No way to recover from a wrong step (closing one device too early) without the county
  (H3 user control; NN/g undo over confirmation, but irreversible steps confirmed).
- Reports that don't print clearly or can't be compared with the paper log at a glance.
- Error messages that don't say whether it's the worker's action or a device fault
  (7.3-K).

**Walkthrough tasks:** Zero report and open; replace a failed BMD mid-day and account
for its ballots; multi-day early-voting suspend and resume (8.4-A task c); close,
reconcile counts and print reports.

**Recruiting profile:** Experienced poll workers/leads (5+ elections). 8.4-A: range of
experience — high end.
