# S07 — Dr. Nkechi Obi, 49, post-election auditor

**Snapshot:** Statistician working with the state's audit team on risk-limiting audits
(RLAs). Compares hand counts of sampled paper ballots against the voting system's
records.

**Context:** Public audit sessions with observers; audit boards of volunteers read
paper ballots aloud while she checks the system's records for the same ballots.

**Goals:** Retrieve the exact system record for each sampled ballot, compare, document
discrepancies, and explain the result to the public.

**Knowledge:** Statistics and audit methods — expert. Election system internals —
moderate. English-only reader for this work.

**Language & literacy:** English (also Igbo); reads ballots in other languages only
through their English contest-option text (5.1-C).

**Abilities & assistive tech:** None.

**Devices & modes:** Cast-vote-record (CVR) exports, ballot manifests, ballot images,
audit software, printed ballots.

**Behaviours:** Exports everything to CSV and analyses it in her own tools; distrusts
anything she can't export.

**What breaks for them:**
- Paper records in other languages without English contest-option text (5.1-C).
- CVR and ballot IDs that can't be matched to physical ballots (batch, position).
- Exports in proprietary formats, missing columns, or summary-only data.
- Reports whose terms differ between screens and exports.
- Images and records that don't make clear what the system interpreted for each mark.

**Walkthrough tasks:** For 5 sampled ballot IDs, find the paper ballot location and the
system's CVR, compare, record one discrepancy, export the audit log.

**Recruiting profile:** Audit staff or academic auditors who have run an RLA or ballot
comparison audit. Not in 8.4-A — test the audit/export functions separately.
