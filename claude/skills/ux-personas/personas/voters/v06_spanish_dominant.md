# V06 — Lucía Hernández, 46, Spanish-dominant

**Snapshot:** Restaurant manager, naturalised citizen 10 years ago. Fluent and fast in
Spanish (phone, banking, news all in Spanish), conversational spoken English, slow and
unsure reading formal English.

**Context:** Votes in a jurisdiction covered for Spanish under Voting Rights Act
Section 203. Her teenage son often offers to "help", which she doesn't want in the
booth.

**Goals:** Vote entirely in Spanish, understand the ballot measures, and be sure the
Spanish and English versions mean the same thing.

**Knowledge:** Civic — moderate; follows local news in Spanish. Tech — high on her phone.

**Language & literacy:** Spanish native, high literacy. English: limited reading
proficiency, especially legal phrasing.

**Abilities & assistive tech:** None.

**Devices & modes:** BMD, visual mode, Spanish.

**Behaviours:** Looks for "Español" immediately. If the start screen is English-only,
she hesitates and asks a poll worker. Compares Spanish text with English candidate
names and party labels.

**What breaks for them:**
- Language switch not visible on every screen, or labelled "Spanish" instead of
  "Español" (7.3-M).
- Partial translation: warnings, help, timeout alerts, review screen or error messages
  left in English (5.2-B, 5.2-C).
- Switching language mid-session resets selections or position (5.2-F).
- Literal or machine translation of ballot measures; inconsistent terms across screens.
- Layout breaking with Spanish text (~20–30 % longer): truncated buttons, overflow.
- Party names, office titles or instructions not localised where the law allows.
- Printed record she can't read (and 5.1-C: English still needed on it for auditors —
  both must work).

**Walkthrough tasks:** Choose Spanish on the first screen, vote, read a ballot measure,
hit an undervote warning, switch to English on review to check a name, switch back.

**Recruiting profile:** Native Spanish speaker, prefers Spanish materials, limited
English reading. 8.3-A 1a: native speakers of each supported language.
