# V10 — Emma Okafor, 29, traumatic brain injury

**Snapshot:** Former paramedic, TBI from a car accident three years ago. Intelligent
and well-informed; short-term memory, attention and processing speed are affected.
Fatigues quickly in noisy places.

**Context:** Votes early on a quiet morning to avoid crowds. Brings a written list of
her choices.

**Goals:** Match her list to the ballot without losing track, and be sure every choice
was recorded.

**Knowledge:** Civic — high. Tech — moderate-high, though complex apps tire her.

**Language & literacy:** English native, good reader when not fatigued.

**Abilities & assistive tech:** Forgets where she was after an interruption; struggles
with multi-part instructions and time pressure; overwhelmed by visual clutter and
flashing or animated elements.

**Devices & modes:** BMD, visual mode; may use audio + visual for long measures.

**Behaviours:** Works one contest at a time, checking her paper list. Gets anxious at
timers and warnings. Re-reads the review screen several times.

**What breaks for them:**
- Several instructions in one block; steps not separated (7.3-K separate steps).
- No clear "where am I" (contest 4 of 12) and no stable layout between contests
  (NN/g: show the current step; H1 visibility of status).
- Inactivity alert that's abrupt, alarming or short (7.2-O 20–45 s, plain copy).
- Animation, flashing, auto-advancing screens.
- Review screen that's dense or that requires memory of earlier screens to interpret.
- Error messages that don't say what to do next.

**Walkthrough tasks:** Vote a 12-contest ballot from a written list, get interrupted
(simulate 2 minutes idle) mid-way, respond to the inactivity alert, review and cast.

**Recruiting profile:** Self-reported cognitive or memory disability (TBI, ADHD, MCI).
Not a named 8.3-A group, but covered by the general-population sample — recruit
deliberately.
