# V02 — Dorothy Lindqvist, 78, low vision (macular degeneration)

**Snapshot:** Retired schoolteacher, has voted in every election since 1968. Age-related
macular degeneration has taken her central vision in one eye and blurred the other.

**Context:** Votes on a weekday morning at her precinct, a neighbour drives her. Proud
of voting independently; hates asking for help.

**Goals:** Read the whole ballot herself, at her own pace, without anyone seeing her
choices.

**Knowledge:** Civic — high, reads the voter guide in advance. Tech — low-moderate:
uses an iPad with large text and a magnifier app; distrusts touchscreens.

**Language & literacy:** English native, strong reader — when the text is big enough.

**Abilities & assistive tech:** Needs large text (her limit: roughly 2× default) and
high contrast; peripheral vision means she loses track of where she is on a zoomed
screen. Glare from windows and overhead lights hurts. Mild hand stiffness.

**Devices & modes:** BMD, enhanced visual: large text + high contrast; sometimes
audio with headphones as a backup for long ballot measures.

**Behaviours:** Leans close to the screen. Looks for the settings first; if she can't
find the text-size control quickly, she asks a poll worker (which she dislikes). Reads
every word. Scrolling makes her lose her place.

**What breaks for them:**
- Text-size and contrast controls hidden in a menu or labelled with tiny icons only
  (7.1-G, 7.3-M style visibility).
- Enlarged text that truncates, overlaps or forces horizontal scrolling; layout must
  reflow (WCAG 1.4.10).
- Changing size/contrast mid-ballot loses her selections or position (5.2-F).
- Low-contrast secondary text, greyed "disabled-looking" active controls (7.1-C 10:1).
- Selection shown only by a subtle colour change she can't see; needs a strong
  checkmark and border.
- Timeouts that don't allow for slow reading (7.2-O alert must be readable at her
  settings).
- Glossy screen glare: note as Not checked (hardware).

**Walkthrough tasks:** Turn on large text and high contrast, then vote a long ballot with
two ballot measures. Review all selections on the summary screen at large text.

**Recruiting profile:** 65+, low vision (not legally blind), uses magnification or large
text daily. 8.3-A 1c: enhanced visual with and without audio.
