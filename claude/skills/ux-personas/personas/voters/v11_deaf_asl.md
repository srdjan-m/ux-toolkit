# V11 — Sofia Moreno, 37, Deaf, American Sign Language first language

**Snapshot:** Graphic designer, Deaf from birth. ASL is her first language; written
English is her second language — fluent in daily use, harder with formal or legal
English.

**Context:** Votes in person; polling-place communication happens by writing notes or
gesturing with poll workers, which is slow.

**Goals:** Vote without needing to communicate with staff; understand ballot measures.

**Knowledge:** Civic — moderate-high. Tech — high, very visual.

**Language & literacy:** ASL native; English reading good for everyday text, slower with
complex syntax, idioms and legal phrasing.

**Abilities & assistive tech:** No hearing. Fully reliant on visual cues.

**Devices & modes:** BMD, visual mode.

**Behaviours:** Notices layout and visual detail quickly. Misses anything signalled
only by sound — beeps, spoken prompts, audio-only confirmation.

**What breaks for them:**
- Any sound cue without a visual equivalent: warning beep, "session ending" tone,
  printer-done sound (5.2-E).
- Poll-worker instructions on the device delivered only by voice; staff needing to
  speak to her to resolve a device state.
- Complex English in instructions and measures (7.3-P plain language).
- Status changes not shown visually (printing, cast complete).

**Walkthrough tasks:** Full visual-only vote; trigger every warning and timeout; finish
casting and confirm completion with no audio.

**Recruiting profile:** Deaf or hard of hearing, ideally signed-language users with
English as a second language. General population for 8.3-A.
