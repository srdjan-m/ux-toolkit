# V08 — Harold Begay, 74, Navajo (Diné) speaker

**Snapshot:** Rancher, lives on the Navajo Nation. Navajo is his first and everyday
language; he speaks it but doesn't read written Navajo, and reads English with
difficulty.

**Context:** Votes at a chapter-house polling place, often a long drive. Language
assistance for American Indian languages under VRA Section 203 is often oral, because
many speakers don't read the written form.

**Goals:** Hear the ballot in Navajo, vote without a relative translating, be home before
dark.

**Knowledge:** Civic — moderate; strong on tribal and county issues. Tech — low.

**Language & literacy:** Navajo (spoken). English: limited reading. Some terms
(candidate names, offices) have no direct Navajo equivalent and are explained.

**Abilities & assistive tech:** Mild hearing loss; reading glasses.

**Devices & modes:** BMD in audio + visual mode: Navajo audio with English text on
screen, headphones.

**Behaviours:** Listens to everything once, then wants to repeat parts. Relies on the
audio to tell him where he is and what to press. Hesitant with touch; prefers buttons.

**What breaks for them:**
- Audio language selection that requires reading English first; the choice must be
  reachable by audio.
- Audio and screen out of sync, or screen showing English while audio is Navajo with no
  way to match them up (5.2-E sound and visual cues).
- No repeat or pause (7.2-G); ballot measures read too fast with no rate control
  (7.1-K).
- Audio explanations of offices or measures that differ in content from the written
  version (5.2-B equivalence).
- Instructions that rely on reading ("tap the button labelled…") rather than position
  and audio cues.
- Rural constraints: long sessions → inactivity timeout while listening (7.2-O).

**Walkthrough tasks:** Start a session in Navajo audio without reading English, vote two
contests and a measure, repeat a contest's options, review by audio, cast.

**Recruiting profile:** Native speaker of an oral or primarily oral language the
jurisdiction supports; limited English reading. 8.3-A 1a for that language.
