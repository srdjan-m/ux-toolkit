# V03 — Marcus Bell, 41, blind

**Snapshot:** Software accessibility tester, blind since birth. Expert screen-reader user
(JAWS, VoiceOver at high speech rates). Reads braille.

**Context:** Votes in person to vote privately; previously had to dictate choices to a
poll worker and resents it. Low tolerance for poorly built audio interfaces.

**Goals:** Vote completely privately and as fast as a sighted voter; verify his own
selections before casting.

**Knowledge:** Civic — high. Tech — expert: expects keyboard-like efficiency, skipping,
and rate control.

**Language & literacy:** English native; braille literate (Grade 2).

**Abilities & assistive tech:** No usable vision. Brings his own headphones sometimes
(hygiene). Listens at ~300+ wpm on his own devices.

**Devices & modes:** BMD in audio-only mode with the audio-tactile interface (keypad
with tactile/braille labels), headphones. Screen off for privacy (6.1-C).

**Behaviours:** Learns the keypad layout in the first minute, then moves fast. Skips
instructions once he knows them. Turns speech rate to maximum. Wants to jump to the
next contest without hearing all options.

**What breaks for them:**
- Missing audio commands: pause/resume, repeat, skip to next/previous contest, skip a
  ballot measure's text (7.2-G).
- Speech rate range too narrow or default too slow with no way to change it (7.1-K).
- Audio that doesn't announce how many options a contest has, or which are already
  selected (7.3-C).
- Any feature that exists only visually — a pop-up warning, a review screen, a write-in
  keyboard — without full audio equivalence (5.1-A, 5.2-E).
- Overvote/undervote warnings played through a speaker instead of headphones (6.1-B).
- Keypad labels that aren't tactilely distinct; needing a poll worker to switch modes.
- Paper record he can't verify: is there an audio read-back of the printed record?

**Walkthrough tasks:** Vote audio-only: change rate, skip a ballot measure's full text,
make a write-in, review, change one vote, cast. Turn the screen off and back on.

**Recruiting profile:** Blind (no functional vision), uses a screen reader daily, braille
optional. 8.3-A 1b: audio format plus tactile controls.
