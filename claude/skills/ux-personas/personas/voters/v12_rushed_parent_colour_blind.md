# V12 — Kevin O'Brien, 44, rushed parent, red-green colour-blind

**Snapshot:** Sales manager. Votes on his lunch break with a restless 4-year-old.
Deuteranopia (red-green colour blindness), like about 1 in 12 men of Northern European
ancestry.

**Context:** Constantly interrupted. Wants to be in and out in five minutes.

**Goals:** Vote fast, be confident nothing got skipped by accident.

**Knowledge:** Civic — moderate; knows his top-of-ticket choices, decides the rest on
the spot. Tech — high.

**Language & literacy:** English native, skims.

**Abilities & assistive tech:** Can't reliably tell red from green or see red text on
dark backgrounds; status shown only as red/green is invisible to him.

**Devices & modes:** BMD, visual mode.

**Behaviours:** Looks away mid-task, comes back and taps whatever is highlighted. Scans
the review screen in seconds. Takes shortcuts and skips "optional" screens.

**What breaks for them:**
- Selected vs unselected, error vs ok, or contest status (complete / undervoted) shown
  by colour only (7.1-F, WCAG 1.4.1).
- Red/green status pairs; warning messages in red on dark backgrounds.
- Losing position after an interruption; screens that time out quickly (7.2-O).
- A review screen where undervoted contests don't stand out without colour.
- Primary button placement that changes between screens, causing mis-taps when fast.

**Walkthrough tasks:** Vote fast with two simulated interruptions; skip one contest by
accident; find it on the review screen with colour removed (greyscale test).

**Recruiting profile:** Colour vision deficiency (screen with an Ishihara test); time-
pressured voters in general. General population for 8.3-A.
