# V07 — Wei Zhang, 68, Mandarin-dominant (reads Traditional or Simplified Chinese)

**Snapshot:** Retired civil engineer, naturalised citizen. Highly educated; reads
technical Chinese fluently, reads English slowly with a dictionary app.

**Context:** Votes in a Section 203 jurisdiction covered for Chinese. Cares about
local measures (zoning, transit).

**Goals:** Read the full ballot and measures in Chinese, and recognise candidates
whose names he knows in Chinese from community press.

**Knowledge:** Civic — high on local issues, moderate on US procedures (judicial
retention, party primaries). Tech — moderate.

**Language & literacy:** Mandarin native; reads Chinese characters (prefers one script
— jurisdictions differ on Traditional vs Simplified, and on dialect for audio:
Mandarin vs Cantonese). English: limited.

**Abilities & assistive tech:** Presbyopia (reading glasses), mild hearing loss.

**Devices & modes:** BMD, visual mode, Chinese; audio Chinese possible.

**Behaviours:** Looks for "中文". Expects family name first. Reads every measure in full.

**What breaks for them:**
- Language list in English only ("Chinese") instead of native names "中文" (7.3-M).
- Font without full CJK coverage: missing glyphs (tofu boxes), mixed fallback fonts,
  CJK text too small to read at the same px size as Latin text.
- Candidate names: transliterated vs Chinese-character names (follow the jurisdiction's
  rules), name order, alignment of names with differing lengths.
- Line breaking and justification rules that break CJK text badly; truncated labels.
- Audio in the wrong dialect, or only partially translated.
- Dates and numbers in formats that read ambiguously.

**Walkthrough tasks:** Select 中文, read a long ballot measure, vote a contest with
Chinese-character candidate names, review, cast. Repeat with large text.

**Recruiting profile:** Native Chinese reader (match the jurisdiction's script and audio
dialect), limited English. 8.3-A 1a.
